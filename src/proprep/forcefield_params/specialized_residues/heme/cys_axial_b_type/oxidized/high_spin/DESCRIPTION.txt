NOS oxygenase-domain heme — ferric (Fe³⁺, S=5/2, resting oxidized).

This set covers the heme + proximal Cys415 (rat nNOS) thiolate-ligated
oxygenase-domain b-type heme. Parameters fit specifically for the
substrate (L-Arg)-bound active-site geometry; force constants are
calibrated to that conformational basin.

Residues:
    HTO  — heme cofactor (Fe + porphyrin macrocycle; 33 heavy + 22 H)
    CTO  — proximal Cys415 thiolate (10 atoms; ff14SB-compatible backbone)

Method: Yang 2016 HEB macrocycle + Seminario Fe-shell from B3LYP/[LANL2DZ+6-31G(d)]
QM Hessian; Shahrokh 2012 IC6 Cys-axial-specific dihedrals (published P450
Cys-axial values, applicable to NOS without modification). Yang/Capece 2013
BBA Fe Lennard-Jones (1.20 Å, 0.05 kcal/mol).

Cross-residue bonds the transformer must emit:
    bond <HTO_resi>.FE <CTO_resi>.SG      ! Fe-S coordination
    bond <HTO_resi>.C2A <PRN_A_resi>.CA   ! propionate A attachment
    bond <HTO_resi>.C3D <PRN_D_resi>.CA   ! propionate D attachment

Net charge: HTO + CTO + 2 PRN = 0 e (Fe³⁺ + porph²⁻ + Cys-thiolate; propionates
are −1 each but ProPrep applies constph PRN library separately).

Limitations:
  * Substrate-bound geometry only. For apoenzyme / NO-released states the
    force constants are still chemically valid but calibrated to the
    substrate-bound basin.
  * No distal axial ligand (5-coordinate Cys-axial only). For 6-coordinate
    Fe-O₂, Fe-NO, Fe-CO states, additional parameterization is needed.
  * Fe LJ uses Yang's 1.20/0.05; for non-bonded contact at the distal face
    after substrate release, the choice should be revisited.

References: see docs/nos_heme_methodology.md and the per-set methodology
field in metadata.json.
