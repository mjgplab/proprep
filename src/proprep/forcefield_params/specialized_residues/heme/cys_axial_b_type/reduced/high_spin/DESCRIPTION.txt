NOS oxygenase-domain heme — ferrous (Fe²⁺, S=2, one-electron reduced).

This set covers the heme + proximal Cys415 (rat nNOS) thiolate-ligated
oxygenase-domain b-type heme. Parameters fit specifically for the
substrate (L-Arg)-bound active-site geometry; force constants are
calibrated to that conformational basin.

Residues:
    HTR  — heme cofactor (Fe + porphyrin macrocycle; 33 heavy + 22 H)
    CTR  — proximal Cys415 thiolate (10 atoms; ff14SB-compatible backbone)

Method: Yang 2016 HEB macrocycle + Seminario Fe-shell from B3LYP/[LANL2DZ+6-31G(d)]
QM Hessian; Shahrokh 2012 IC6 Cys-axial-specific dihedrals (published P450
Cys-axial values, applicable to NOS without modification). Yang/Capece 2013
BBA Fe Lennard-Jones (1.20 Å, 0.05 kcal/mol).

State-distinguishing parameters (vs ferric):
    Fe-N    2.15–2.16 Å  (vs 2.08–2.10 Å ferric)  — HS Fe(III)→HS Fe(II) shift
    Fe-S    2.45 Å       (vs 2.36 Å ferric)
    Heme.FE charge  +0.520 (vs +0.727 ferric)
    Cys.SG charge   −0.523 (vs −0.387 ferric)

Cross-residue bonds the transformer must emit:
    bond <HTR_resi>.FE <CTR_resi>.SG      ! Fe-S coordination
    bond <HTR_resi>.C2A <PRN_A_resi>.CA   ! propionate A attachment
    bond <HTR_resi>.C3D <PRN_D_resi>.CA   ! propionate D attachment

Net charge: HTR + CTR + 2 PRN = −1 e (Fe²⁺ + porph²⁻ + Cys-thiolate, plus
propionates handled by constph PRN). tleap will report one expected
"unit charge −1.0 is not zero" warning.

Limitations: same as the ferric set; see oxidized/default/DESCRIPTION.txt
and docs/nos_heme_methodology.md.
