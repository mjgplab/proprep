Cytochrome c2 heme — ferrous (Fe²⁺, low-spin S=0 closed-shell, reduced).

His/Met-axial c-type heme of cyt c2 (Blastochloris/Rhodopseudomonas viridis):
six-coordinate, His(NE2) + Met(SD) axial ligands, covalently bound to the
protein through two thioether linkages (CXXCH motif). One-electron-reduced
counterpart of the ferric (HMO) set; pair them for redox / charge-compensation
work.

Units in this lib (combined OFF — SELF-CONTAINED, like the bis-His c-type set):
    HMR  — heme macrocycle + axial His imidazole + axial Met thioether
           + the two CXXCH thioether-Cys sidechains (87 atoms; net 0 e)
    CYO  — thioether-Cys backbone stub (used ×2)
    HIO  — axial-His backbone stub
    MEO  — axial-Met backbone stub (N/H/CA/HA/C/O; 6 atoms; net 0 e)
    PRN  — propionate (used ×2; -1 each)

This set loads with any standard AMBER protein FF (ff14SB/parm10 or ff19SB/parm19 —
the reused standard types are parameter-identical in both) + an `addAtomTypes`
registration of the custom heme types (FR, NP, NO, CD, Cp — see addAtomTypes.leap /
LOADING.md; REQUIRED, else tleap mis-assigns GB Born radii for the NO-typed pyrrole
N) — no leaprc.conste, no leaprc.constph. All splice stubs are bundled above, and
PRN's protonated-carboxyl angles/impropers are folded into the .frcmod.

Method: identical protocol to the ferric set, on the Fe²⁺ S=0 state.
State-specific differences: Fe-Npyrrole softer/longer (K 65.15 vs 76.70);
Fe-S(Met) markedly softer and longer (K 12.22 vs 33.33 — labile ferrous
Fe-S(Met)). Charges: CM5 (density-based); the redox change vs ferric is the
clean CM5 density difference (~ -0.5 e at Fe), not a pinned Mulliken value
(see metadata.json methodology and methodology.md §13).

Charge-set alternatives (4 reduced libs sharing ONE bonded frcmod —
Guberman_HMR.frcmod; bonded terms are charge-independent):
    Guberman_HMR_CM5.lib           — CM5 (default; recommended)
    Guberman_HMR_RESPmc.lib        — pooled multi-conformer RESP
    Guberman_HMR_RESPsc.lib        — single-conformer RESP
    Guberman_HMR_CM5_FeLocal.lib   — CM5 charges with the redox Δq localized
                                     entirely on Fe (Blumberger-style; the
                                     −1.0 e endpoint of the comparison axis)
Redox-Δq sensitivity axis (Fe Δq −0.15 → −0.50 → −1.0 e); methodology.md §13.12.
CM5_FeLocal is an intentionally crude limiting case for the sensitivity study,
NOT a recommended production charge model.

CORRECTION 2026-08-02 - trans pyrrole N-Fe-N angles
---------------------------------------------------
Same defect as the ferric set: NP and NO alternate around the macrocycle
(NA=NP, NB=NO, NC=NP, ND=NO), so a same-type triplet is a TRANS pair. All three
N-Fe-N combinations had been written with the cis class average 109.99/90.000,
restraining two ~178 deg angles to 90 deg. Trajectories produced with the
previous values are invalid.

The original ferrous QM was not recoverable (only a 2024 BLYP/6-31G(d)
all-electron job survived, a different functional and core treatment that must
not be mixed with this fit). The calculation was therefore REPRODUCED at the
ferric level of theory: B3LYP/[LANL2DZ Fe + 6-31G(d)], charge 0, multiplicity 1,
opt freq, geometry from 1CO6 chain A (R. viridis ferrocytochrome c2, the
species-matched partner to the ferric 1IO3), same sidechain-only model with H
caps and the four sidechain CB Cartesian-frozen at crystal positions.

The reproduction is faithful, which is what makes this a correction rather than
a replacement. Optimised geometry against the shipped values:

    Fe-N (axial His)    2.0158   vs  2.0158
    Fe-N pyrrole mean   2.0265   vs  2.0265
    Fe-S (axial Met)    2.4952   vs  2.4955
    N(His)-Fe-S       176.006    vs 176.020

and the Seminario refit (pymsmt, scalef=1.000, avg=1) against the shipped force
constants:

    FR-NP     65.14  vs  65.15        NB-FR-S      93.96  vs  93.96
    FR-NB     54.60  vs  54.61        NB-FR-Npyr  105.02  vs 105.00
    FR-S      12.25  vs  12.22        cis          109.98 vs 109.99

Every unaffected term reproduces to within rounding, so the rest of this file is
validated rather than assumed, and only the two trans lines changed:

    NP-FR-NP     90.15  178.756     ! trans NA-Fe-NC
    NO-FR-NO    101.57  178.196     ! trans NB-Fe-ND

NP vs NO assignment was fixed independently on this geometry by thioether
orientation: ring B's thioether faces ring C, ring C's faces ring D. Composition
alone cannot decide it, since both trans pairs hold one thioether and one
propionate ring.

No other term changed. Charges are unaffected. The job that produced this is at
/Users/mjgp/Desktop/Software/cytc2_ferrous (normal termination, NImag=0).
