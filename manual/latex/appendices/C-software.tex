\chapter{Which features need extra software}
\label{app:software}

The ProPrep installer brings ProPrep, AmberTools (tLEaP, sander, cpptraj,
ParmEd, antechamber, MCPB.py and others) and the helper programs for
protonation (PROPKA, pdb2pqr), membranes (packmol-memgen with MEMEMBED and
PPM3), docking (AutoDock Vina, AutoGrid) and structure alignment (tmtools). The
table below lists, tool by tool, what else is needed and what happens without
it. ``Nothing extra'' means the installer has everything.

What was tested: our test computer had no MODELLER key, no Gaussian, no ORCA,
no CREST or xtb, no \texttt{pmemd} and no graphics card. The ``Without it''
column describes what we saw there, unless it says otherwise.

\section{The extra programs in short}
\label{sec:software-programs}

\begin{description}
  \item[MODELLER license key.] Free for academic users from\newline
        \url{https://salilab.org/modeller/registration.html}.\newline MODELLER itself
        is installed with ProPrep. Section~\ref{sec:installation-modeller}
        shows how to add the key.
  \item[Gaussian 16.] Commercial quantum-chemistry program, licensed by your
        institution. ProPrep writes its input files; you run them with
        \texttt{g16}.
  \item[ORCA.] Quantum-chemistry program from the ORCA developers, with its
        own license. ProPrep needs \texttt{orca\_mm} from it to prepare ORCA
        QM/MM inputs.
  \item[pmemd.] The fast simulation engine of Amber, distributed separately
        from the free AmberTools. \texttt{pmemd.cuda} runs on NVIDIA graphics
        cards.
  \item[CREST and xtb.] Programs for searching molecular conformations, used
        by one optional refinement in \key{2c}.
\end{description}
