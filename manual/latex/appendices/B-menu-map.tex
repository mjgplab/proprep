\chapter{Menu map}\label{app:menumap}

This appendix lists every key in ProPrep~1.23.0's menus: the main menu, the
settings keys, the first-level choices inside each tool, and where each tool
sits in Workflow Mode. Section~\ref{sec:menus-main} explains how to read the
menus.

\section{Main menu (Full Menu)}\label{sec:menumap-main}

Type the key at \prompt{Enter your choice (1a)}. \key{Enter} alone opens
\key{1a}.

\begin{longtable}{@{}p{0.07\linewidth}p{0.28\linewidth}p{0.47\linewidth}p{0.08\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Key} & \textbf{Tool} & \textbf{What it is for} & \textbf{Ch.} \\
\midrule
\endhead
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textsf{\bfseries 1. STRUCTURE INPUT}} \\
\key{1a} & Structure Loader & Get a structure: PDB code, search, AlphaFold,
AlphaFill or a local file; also Amber files and ligand files. &
\ref{ch:structure} \\
\key{1b} & Biological Assembly Generator & Build the working (biological) form
of a molecule from the crystal's asymmetric unit. & \ref{ch:structure} \\
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textsf{\bfseries 2. REDOX SITE DETECTION}} \\
\key{2a} & Redox Site Detector & Find hemes, iron--sulfur clusters, flavins,
metals and disulfides. & \ref{ch:redox} \\
\key{2b} & Force Field Explorer & Look up atom types, residues and parameters
in a force field. & \ref{ch:forcefields} \\
\key{2c} & Force Field Parameterizer & Make parameters for ligands, modified
amino acids and metal sites. & \ref{ch:forcefields} \\
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textsf{\bfseries 3. STRUCTURE COMPARISON}} \\
\key{3a} & Homology Searcher & BLAST search for similar sequences. &
\ref{ch:compare} \\
\key{3b} & Structure Aligner & Superimpose structures and compare them
(RMSD). & \ref{ch:compare} \\
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textsf{\bfseries 4. STRUCTURE PREPARATION}} \\
\key{4a} & PDB Filter & Keep the chains and components you want; remove
waters, ions, ligands. & \ref{ch:cleaning} \\
\key{4b} & Amino Acid Mutator & Change residues. & \ref{ch:cleaning} \\
\key{4c} & Structure Fixer & Find missing residues and atoms; add end caps;
repair (needs MODELLER). & \ref{ch:cleaning} \\
\key{4d} & Redox Site Preparer & Give redox sites the right names and
oxidation states. & \ref{ch:redox} \\
\key{4e} & Protonation State Analyzer & Predict pKa values and set residue
charge states for your pH. & \ref{ch:protonation} \\
\key{4f} & Structure Orientator & Rotate and move the structure onto the x, y
and z axes. & \ref{ch:protonation} \\
\key{4g} & Membrane Builder & Pack a protein into a lipid bilayer, or build a
membrane alone. & \ref{ch:membrane} \\
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textsf{\bfseries 5. SIMULATION SETUP \& EXECUTION}} \\
\key{5a} & Topology Generator & Build the simulation system (topology and
coordinates) with water and ions. & \ref{ch:topology} \\
\key{5b} & Molecular Dynamics Manager & Set up, run, monitor and analyze
simulations. & \ref{ch:runningmd}, \ref{ch:analysis} \\
\key{5c} & QM/MM Preparator & Write Gaussian ONIOM or ORCA QM/MM input files.
& \ref{ch:qmmm} \\
\key{5d} & Molecular Docking & Dock a ligand with AutoDock Vina. &
\ref{ch:docking} \\
\multicolumn{4}{@{}l}{\textsf{\bfseries UTILITIES}} \\
\key{ua} & Structure Viewer & Show structures (and trajectories) in the 3D
viewer in your browser. & \ref{ch:compare} \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\section{Inside each tool}\label{sec:menumap-tools}

These are the numbered choices on each tool's own menu. Every tool menu also
offers \key{b}, \key{m} and \key{x}. A choice that cannot be used yet shows
\texttt{○} with a \texttt{⚠} reason; one that has been done shows \texttt{●}.

\begin{longtable}{@{}p{0.24\linewidth}p{0.70\linewidth}@{}}
\toprule
\textbf{Tool} & \textbf{Choices} \\
\midrule
\endhead
\key{1a} Structure Loader & 1 Load protein structure \textperiodcentered{}
2 Load AMBER topology \& coordinate files \textperiodcentered{} 3 View
structure metadata \textperiodcentered{} 4 Load a ligand file or library
(SDF/mol2/SMILES) for docking.\newline
\textit{Inside 1, the sources:} 1 Protein Data Bank (RCSB), 2 AlphaFold,
3 AlphaFill, 4 local PDB file, 5 view local files together, 6 Done.
\textit{RCSB methods:} 1 PDB ID, 2 title keyword, 3 gene or protein
(UniProt), 4 ligand, 5 back. \\
\key{1b} Biological Assembly & 1 Generate biological assembly from asymmetric
unit. Then: an assembly number, \key{m} (manual matrix; stops ProPrep in
1.23.0) or \key{c} cancel. \\
\key{2a} Redox Site Detector & 1 Import redox sites from JSON file
\textperiodcentered{} 2 Detect redox-active sites \textperiodcentered{} 3 View
detected site summaries \textperiodcentered{} 4 Edit detected sites. \\
\key{2b} Force Field Explorer & 1 Overview \textperiodcentered{} 2 Browse Atom
Types \textperiodcentered{} 3 Browse Residues \textperiodcentered{} 4 Search
Parameters \textperiodcentered{} 5 Reverse Lookup \textperiodcentered{} 6 Load
Different Force Field \textperiodcentered{} 7 Type Structure Atoms

5 Display detailed help information. \\
\key{3a} Homology Searcher & 1 Run BLAST homology search \textperiodcentered{}
2 View BLAST results \textperiodcentered{} 3 Retrieve AlphaFold structures for
hits \textperiodcentered{} 4 Export alignments and annotations
\textperiodcentered{} 5 Build homology model (MODELLER). \\
\key{3b} Structure Aligner & 1 Align multiple PDB structures
\textperiodcentered{} 2 Align structures using redox site residues
\textperiodcentered{} 3 View alignment results. \\
\key{4a} PDB Filter & 1 Filter PDB structure \textperiodcentered{} 2 Show

\key{4b} Amino Acid Mutator & 1 Display protein sequences with numbering
\textperiodcentered{} 2 Specify amino acid mutations \textperiodcentered{}

\key{4c} Structure Fixer & 1 Analyze structure completeness
\textperiodcentered{} 2 Apply repairs/mutations/caps to structure
\textperiodcentered{} 3 View structural issues report \textperiodcentered{}

\key{4d} Redox Site Preparer & 1 Create transformer (interactive PDB editor)
\textperiodcentered{} 2 Generate single redox microstate

\key{4e} Protonation State Analyzer & 1 Analyze protonation states (PROPKA)
\textperiodcentered{} 2 Calculate ideal charge capacitance

\key{5a} Topology Generator & 1 Edit redox site bond definitions
